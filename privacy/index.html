Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Ear for Omi is an iPhone app that works with an Omi pendant and sends your recordings to a backend you set up yourself. The developer does not run any server and does not collect, store or analyze any of your data. There is no account, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDK. The only exception is a problem report you send yourself from the app (see below).

What the app handles, and where it goes

  • Audio from the pendant: travels over Bluetooth to your iPhone, is kept temporarily on the phone, and is then sent to the backend you paired the app with.
  • Transcripts, topics, to-dos, memories, people and similar content: are read from your own backend. Part of it is cached on your phone; the cache has a size limit and the oldest items are removed first.
  • Location (optional, only if you allow it): your footprint is kept on your phone and the whole trail is sent to your own backend (your second brain). The map and place-name lookups use Apple’s map services; when a lookup is made, the coordinates are sent to Apple.
  • Speech recognition (optional): the live draft transcript is produced on your phone with the speech recognition built into iOS.
  • Notifications: only reminders scheduled locally on your phone: to-dos that are due, and a reminder when recording is interrupted.

Your own backend

How your backend handles data is the responsibility of whoever set it up. It is outside the scope of this policy.

Reporting a problem

Only when you tap Send in the app do we receive, in our Google spreadsheet, the problem you describe, any contact details you choose to add, the app version, system version, device model and interface language, and a random ID created at install that is used only to limit how many reports can be sent. We use them to look into the problem and reply to you. No photos, recordings, location, advertising identifier or data from the app are sent.

Recording other people

Recordings may capture other people. You are responsible for following the recording and personal-data laws that apply where you are, and for getting the other person’s consent when it is required.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data on your phone. Data on your backend has to be deleted on your own backend.

Children

Ear for Omi is not designed for children under 13.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits, see which pages are read and how many people tap the App Store link. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies only to the website: the app does not include Google Analytics. You can block it in your browser’s privacy settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Apple

When you install the app through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple handles installation, crash and similar data under its own policies.
