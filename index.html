An independent iPhone app for the Omi pendant

What your pendant hears goes to your own backend.

Ear for Omi receives audio from your Omi pendant over Bluetooth and sends it to a backend you set up yourself. Topics, to-dos and memories organized by that backend come back to your phone. The developer runs no server and never handles your data.

What it does

An iPhone client for the pendant. Recording, organizing and storage happen on your own backend.

  • Pendant audio over BluetoothConnects to your Omi pendant and receives its audio. When the connection drops, audio is kept on the phone and sent once it is back. Recordings the pendant made while offline are synced too.
  • Live transcriptA draft transcript appears while you talk, recognized offline on your phone.
  • TopicsYour backend organizes each day’s conversations into topics. Browse by day, and open a topic for its summary, transcript and recording.
  • Daily review, to-dos, memories, peopleLook back on the day, keep to-dos (recurring ones and due reminders included), and read the memories and people your backend keeps.
  • The day’s footprint mapSee where you were that day. Location is stored only on your phone, and only if you choose to turn it on.
  • Button, widget and Lock ScreenVoice commands from the pendant button, and mute control from a widget and the Lock Screen.

What you need

Ear for Omi does not work on its own.

An Omi pendant

The wearable recording device made by Based Hardware. Ear for Omi connects to it over Bluetooth.

Your own compatible backend

A recording backend and a “second brain” knowledge service. The first time you open the app both addresses are empty; you fill them in yourself.

Demo mode

The app includes a demo mode with a day of sample data. It is a way to look around the interface, not a substitute for a backend.

Your data stays with you

  • The developer runs no server and does not collect, store or analyze your data
  • No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDK
  • Audio is sent only to the backend address you enter in Settings
  • Your footprint stays on your phone, and only exists if you turn location on

Questions

Is this the official Omi app?

No. Ear for Omi is an independent, third-party client. It is not affiliated with Based Hardware.

How is it different from the official app?

Recordings and data go to a backend you set up yourself. The developer has no server and never handles your data.

Can I use it without a backend?

No. Without your own backend the app can only show its built-in demo data. Please make sure you have a backend before you buy.

Where do I get the backend?

The backend does not come with the app. It is shared through a community discussion; how to join will be posted on the support page. Until then, you can email us.
