What your pendant hears becomes your day.And it stays on your own backend.

Ear for Omi is an iPhone app for the Omi pendant. Recordings go to a backend you set up yourself, which turns them into topics, to-dos and memories and sends them back to your phone.

And also

  • The day’s footprint mapWhere you were that day. Location is stored only on your phone, and only if you turn it on.
  • Live transcriptA draft appears on your phone while you talk, using the speech recognition built into iOS.
  • Catches up after a dropoutWhen the backend can’t be reached, audio waits on the phone and is sent later. Recordings the pendant made offline are synced too.
  • Voice commands from the buttonPress the pendant button and speak: ask about the past, add a to-do, or have something remembered.
  • Widget and Lock ScreenMute and unmute from a widget or the Lock Screen without opening the app.
  • Demo modeA day of sample data and sample recordings is built in, so you can look around. It does not replace a backend.

Your data stays with you

  • The developer runs no serverNothing of yours is collected, stored or analyzed.
  • No account, ads or trackingNo sign-up, and no analytics or tracking SDK in the app.
  • Audio goes to one placeOnly to the backend address you enter in Settings.

What the backend is, and how to get it

Ear for Omi does not organize anything by itself. What the pendant records is handled by two services you run yourself: a recording backend turns audio into transcripts, and a “second brain” turns transcripts into topics, to-dos and memories. The backend does not come with the app; email us and we will send you the code and the setup guide.

Questions

Is this the official Omi app?

No. Ear for Omi is an independent, third-party client. It is not affiliated with Based Hardware.

How is it different from the official app?

Recordings and data go to a backend you set up yourself. The developer has no server and never handles your data.

Can I use it without a backend?

No. Without your own backend the app can only show its built-in demo data. Please make sure you have a backend before you buy.

Where do I get the backend?

The backend does not come with the app. Email the support address and we will send you the code and the setup guide. How it works and what you need are on the Backend page.

How much is it?

US$139, a one-time purchase with no subscription. It is currently available only on the Taiwan App Store, where the price is shown in New Taiwan dollars.

What language is the app in?

The app’s interface is currently in Traditional Chinese only.
