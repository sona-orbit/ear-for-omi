Common questions

What do I need?

An Omi pendant, and your own compatible backend: a recording backend and a “second brain” knowledge service. Ear for Omi does not come with a backend.

How do I set it up the first time?

Open Settings in the app, enter the WebSocket address of your recording backend and the address of your second brain, then turn off Pause.

Can I use it without a backend?

You can turn on demo mode and look around the interface with sample data. Recording and organizing need your own backend.

The app can’t connect to my pendant.

Check that Ear for Omi is allowed to use Bluetooth, that the pendant is charged, and that the official Omi app is not holding the connection at the same time.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data on your phone. Data on your backend has to be deleted on your own backend.

Is this the official Omi app?

No. Ear for Omi is an independent, third-party client and is not affiliated with Based Hardware.
