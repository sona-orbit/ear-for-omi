Common questions

What do I need?

An Omi pendant, and your own compatible backend: a recording backend and a “second brain” knowledge service. Ear for Omi does not come with a backend.

How do I set it up the first time?

Open Settings in the app, paste your backend’s two addresses into the recording backend field and the second brain field, and tap “套用並檢查” (apply and check) under each. The app checks the connection step by step and tells you what is wrong if a step fails. Once both pass, turn off Pause.

Can I use it without a backend?

No. Without your own backend the app can only show its built-in demo data. Please make sure you have a backend before you buy.

Where do I get the backend?

The backend does not come with the app. Email us and we will send you the code and the setup guide. How it works and what you need are on the Backend page.

The app can’t connect to my pendant.

Check that Ear for Omi is allowed to use Bluetooth, that the pendant is charged, and that the official Omi app is not holding the connection at the same time.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data on your phone. Data on your backend has to be deleted on your own backend.

Is this the official Omi app?

No. Ear for Omi is an independent, third-party client and is not affiliated with Based Hardware.
